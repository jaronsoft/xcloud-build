## 当前 App Store 推广文本

### 简体中文

买之前，先问问大家。添加一件商品或对比两款，设定愿意接受的价格，分享你的问题，看看投票、评论和大家分享的价格，再做自己的决定。

### English (U.S.)

Before you buy, ask around. Add a product or compare two, set a price you'd accept, and share your question for votes, comments, and price tips.

## App Store 描述

### 简体中文

想买这件，还是在两款之间犹豫？先听听大家的看法，再做决定。

填写商品名称；照片、价格和商品链接都可选。你可以发起“买还是不买”，也可以用“二选一”比较两款商品。还可以设定愿意接受的价格，通过专属链接或分享图片邀请大家参与，或申请展示在“发现”页，让更多人看到。

- 持有链接的人都能投票。
- 登录用户可以评论、推荐替代品，或分享看到的价格。
- 随时回看讨论，并记录自己的最终选择。

申请展示在“发现”页的内容经审核通过后才会出现。任何持有分享链接的人都能查看对应决策。商品信息和价格可能变化，请在购买前以商家页面为准。ByeMyBuy 不销售商品，也不处理付款。

### English (U.S.)

Not sure whether to buy it—or which one to choose? Ask others before you decide.

Add a product name, with an optional photo, price, and store link. Ask “Buy or Pass” or compare two products with “Pick One.” You can also set a price you'd accept, share your question by link or image, or submit it to Explore for review.

- Anyone with the link can vote.
- Signed-in members can comment, suggest an alternative, or share a price they found.
- Revisit the discussion and record your final choice.

Explore submissions appear only after review. Anyone holding your share link can view that decision. Product details and prices may change; check the merchant before buying. ByeMyBuy does not sell products or process payments.
