## 1.2.11 — 2026-09-28 (Build 133) — 已发布

### 简体中文

- 画幅比例和清晰度可并排选择，比例选项按方向分组，更方便浏览。
- 常用社媒比例会显示平台图标，点击即可查看适用平台和常见用途。
- 每天会以轻量提示介绍更多画幅比例和更高分辨率选项。
- 模板详情页会显示实际创作点数和优惠信息；暂不可选的规格也会说明提升等级的途径。
- 创作中的预计倒计时显示在作品卡片上，卡片下方改为进度条。
- 在模板搜索框按下搜索键即可查看结果。
- 点击多条提醒的汇总通知，现在会直接打开通知页面。

### English (U.S.)

- Choose an aspect ratio and resolution side by side, with ratios grouped by orientation for easier browsing.
- Popular social media ratios show platform icons and common uses when tapped.
- A subtle daily prompt introduces more aspect ratios and higher resolution options.
- Template details show the actual creation cost and discount, with guidance on how to unlock restricted options.
- Estimated generation time appears on the work card, with a progress bar below.
- Press Search in the template search field to view matching results.
- Tapping a summary of multiple alerts now opens the Notifications page directly.
