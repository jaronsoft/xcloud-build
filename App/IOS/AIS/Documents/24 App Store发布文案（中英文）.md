### 长描述

AIS 产品智造是一款面向个人、品牌与企业的 AI 视觉创作工具。无论你正在准备产品展示、品牌宣传、电商素材、活动海报，还是希望把一个初步想法变成可用画面，AIS 都能帮助你更快开始、更清楚地表达。

你可以从精选模板和案例中获得灵感，上传参考图片或素材，选择适合的创作方向与交付规格，再按自己的目标完成创作。创作完成后，成果会保存在任务中，便于查看、继续修改、保存或分享。

主要功能：

- **模板与案例**：浏览经过筛选的模板和视觉案例，了解所需素材与预期成果，降低创作门槛。
- **快速创作**：通过文字描述、参考图片和可选创作参数生成视觉内容；可用能力会根据你的地区和服务配置显示。
- **图片编辑与优化**：围绕已有图片继续调整画面方向、构图、风格和表达重点，让创作过程更连贯。
- **项目化创作**：在项目中整理目标与素材，获得受控的创作建议、成果计划和明确报价；所有付费创作均需你确认后才会启动。
- **任务与成果管理**：跟踪任务状态，集中查看历史成果，并将需要的内容保存或分享至后续工作流。
- **清晰的使用边界**：创作前展示适用素材、预计消耗和处理说明；生成失败的任务按实际规则处理。

AIS 适合产品展示、品牌内容、营销素材、社交媒体视觉和日常创意表达。AI 生成结果可能存在偏差，请在发布、生产或商业使用前结合实际需求进行审核，并确保你对上传素材和使用成果拥有必要的权利与授权。

### Long Description

AIS Visual Studio by WeKare is an AI visual creation app for individuals, brands, and teams. Whether you are preparing product visuals, brand content, ecommerce assets, campaign artwork, or simply turning an idea into an image, AIS helps you start faster and communicate more clearly.

Explore curated templates and examples for inspiration. Add reference images or assets, choose a visual direction and delivery format, and create around your own goal. Your finished work is kept with its task, making it easy to review, refine, save, or share.

Key features:

- **Templates and examples:** Explore curated templates and visual examples to understand the expected result and the assets you may need.
- **Quick creation:** Create visual content from a description, reference images, and optional creative settings. Available capabilities are shown based on your region and service configuration.
- **Image editing and refinement:** Continue working from an existing image to adjust visual direction, composition, style, and emphasis in a connected workflow.
- **Project-based creation:** Organize goals and assets in a project, receive guided creation suggestions, a result plan, and a clear quote. Any paid creation starts only after your confirmation.
- **Task and result management:** Follow task progress, revisit prior results, and save or share the work you need for your next step.
- **Clear creation controls:** Review applicable assets, estimated usage, and processing information before creating. Failed tasks are handled according to the applicable rules.

AIS is designed for product presentation, brand content, marketing assets, social visuals, and everyday creative expression. AI-generated results can be imperfect. Please review results before publishing, production, or commercial use, and make sure you have the necessary rights and permissions for every asset you upload and use.
